Option 1 — Left-aligned, accent rule

We ran a three-week design sprint with one central question: what are the jobs people actually do here, regardless of the tools they use today? By the end, 90+ scattered components had collapsed into six coherent product domains.

Solutions are ephemeral. Jobs are immutable.

That reframe accomplished three things simultaneously. It defragmented the ecosystem, shifted focus onto real user workflows, and freed the team to design and build in parallel.

Why this works: stays on the same left edge as every other line on the page, so your eye never resets. Reads as part of the argument. Quietest option — best if quotes appear often.
